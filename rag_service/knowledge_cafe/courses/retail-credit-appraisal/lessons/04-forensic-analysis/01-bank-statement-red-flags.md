# Forensic Bank Statement Analysis & Red Flags

## Common Red Flags in Operating Accounts
1. **Salary Window Padding**: Unusually large single credits received 2-3 days before loan application, often funded by cash or short-term personal borrowing.
2. **Circular Transactions & Cheque Swapping**: Equal or near-equal sums debiting and crediting across linked proprietorships or family accounts within 24–48 hours to artificially inflate banking turnover.
3. **Inward Cheque Returns / ECS Bounces**: More than **2 financial outward/inward bounces** in 6 months indicates severe liquidity stress.
4. **Immediate Post-Credit Drainage**: Account balances dropping to near-zero within 24 hours of receiving salary or receivables (lack of retained liquidity buffer).
5. **EOD Balance Erosion**: Average Monthly Balance (AMB) significantly divergent from peak transaction volumes.
