# Credit Mitigants & Pre-Disbursement Sanction Conditions

## Structuring Compensating Mitigants
When an applicant exhibits minor credit policy deviations, the underwriter must stipulate binding mitigants:
1. **Additional Co-Borrower / Guarantor**: Adding an earning spouse or blood relative with pristine credit bureau records.
2. **Escrow / Salary Account Mandate**: Insisting on an automated Standing Instruction (SI) or e-NACH mandate executed directly on the operating salary account.
3. **Collateral Enhancements**: Lowering the sanctioned LTV by demanding additional upfront equity contribution (margin money) or pledging liquid collateral (Fixed Deposits, Sovereign Gold Bonds).

## Pre-Disbursement Conditions (PDC)
- Verification of original title search certificate from bank-empaneled advocate for minimum 30 years.
- Submission of valid Property Tax receipts and building approval sanction plans.
- Written clearance of any outstanding negative remarks on the credit bureau report.
