# Regulatory Risk Caps & Prudential Norms

## RBI Capital Adequacy & Risk Weight Regulations
- The Reserve Bank of India imposes risk weights on commercial bank retail loan assets based on LTV and credit product type:
  - Consumer credit & personal loans: Subject to elevated risk weights (125% to 150%) to discourage unsecured overheating.
  - Housing loans: Favorable risk weights (35% to 50%) when adhering to conservative LTV guidelines.

## Delegated Authority Matrix
Underwriting sanctions must strictly align with the bank's internal Credit Policy and Delegation of Powers:
- Credit Officer: Loans up to ₹25 Lakh.
- Area Credit Manager: Loans up to ₹1 Crore.
- Zonal Credit Committee: Loans up to ₹5 Crore.
- Central Credit Committee / MD & CEO: Loans exceeding ₹5 Crore.
Deviations from standard credit policy (e.g., LTV waiver or CIBIL score cut-off relaxation) must be documented in a Deviation Matrix with compensating mitigants.
