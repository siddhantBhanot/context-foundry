# The 5 Cs of Credit in Retail Lending

## 1. Character (Willingness to Repay)
- Assessed through Credit Bureau Reports (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF Highmark).
- Critical indicators: Days Past Due (DPD) history, settled or written-off flags, frequency of recent loan inquiries (credit hunger).
- Underwriting threshold: CIBIL score $\ge 750$ with zero 30+ DPD in the preceding 12 months.

## 2. Capacity (Ability to Repay)
- Calculated by matching verified net monthly cash flows against all active and proposed debt obligations.
- Metric: Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR).

## 3. Capital (Borrower Equity / Skin in the Game)
- The applicant's net worth, accumulated liquid savings, and down payment contribution toward asset purchase.
- Higher capital stake significantly reduces moral hazard.

## 4. Collateral (Security & Loss Given Default)
- In secured lending (Home Loans, Loan Against Property, Auto Loans), the quality, marketability, clear legal title, and valuation of the pledged security.
- Metric: Loan to Value (LTV).

## 5. Conditions (Macroeconomic & Sector Realities)
- External factors impacting the borrower: industry volatility, employer risk tier (Category A/B/C corporates), and regulatory shifts.
