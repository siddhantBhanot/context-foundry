# Self-Employed Cash Flow & Financial Appraisal

## Financial Documents Required
1. 2 to 3 years audited Financial Statements (Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss Account, Audit Notes).
2. Income Tax Returns (ITR-3 / ITR-4) with Computation of Income.
3. 12 months Business Current Account statements.
4. GST Returns (GSTR-3B monthly and GSTR-1 quarterly).

## Cash Accrual Calculation Formula
For proprietors, partners, and SME directors:

$$\text{Cash Profit Available for Debt Servicing} = \text{Profit After Tax (PAT)} + \text{Depreciation} + \text{Interest on Term Loans} - \text{Drawings/Dividends}$$

### GST to Banking Reconciliation
Cross-validate the turnover declared in audited accounts against:
1. Cumulative turnover reported in monthly GSTR-3B filings.
2. Total legitimate business credit summations in the bank account (excluding loans, capital infusions, and inter-account transfers).
Any variance greater than **15%** requires formal clarification by a Chartered Accountant.
