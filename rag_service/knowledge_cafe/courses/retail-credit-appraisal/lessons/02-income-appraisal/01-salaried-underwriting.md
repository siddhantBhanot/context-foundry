# Salaried Income Underwriting Methodology

## Core Salary Documents
1. **Salary Slips**: Last 3 months payslips detailing earnings, deductions, and employer designation.
2. **Form 16 / 26AS**: Verification of tax deducted at source by the employer against TRACES portal.
3. **Salary Account Bank Statement**: 6 months statement verifying direct NEFT/RTGS salary credits from the employer entity.

## Net Take-Home Salary Computation
$$\text{Eligible Monthly Income} = \text{Gross Salary} - (\text{Provident Fund} + \text{Professional Tax} + \text{Income Tax} + \text{Voluntary Payroll Deductions})$$

### Variable Compensation Rules
- **Monthly Inherent Allowances** (HRA, Special Allowance, Conveyance): 100% eligible.
- **Performance Bonuses & Annual Incentives**: Haircut applied—underwriting standard counts only 50% of the 2-year average bonus amount, converted to a monthly figure, provided payment is regular.
- **Reimbursements & Travel Allowances**: Excluded from eligible income unless backed by statutory salary credits.
