# Stress Testing Borrower Repayment Capacity

## Macroeconomic Stress Factors
In a floating-rate benchmark regime, interest rates fluctuate with monetary policy. Underwriters must stress test applicant cash flows under simulated shocks:

1. **Interest Rate Shock**: Calculate EMI assuming a **+150 to +200 bps hike** in the lending rate. Ensure the stressed FOIR does not exceed 70%.
2. **Income Volatility Shock**: For applicants in cyclical industries (real estate, hospitality, export apparel), apply a **20% haircut** to current declared income.
3. **Co-Applicant Dependency**: In multi-borrower structures, assess whether the primary applicant can comfortably service at least 70% of total debt if the secondary co-borrower faces career interruption.
