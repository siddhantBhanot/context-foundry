# Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR) and Loan to Value (LTV)

## Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR)
$$\text{FOIR} = \frac{\text{Total Existing Monthly EMIs} + \text{Proposed New Loan EMI}}{\text{Eligible Monthly Income}} \times 100$$

### Benchmark FOIR Thresholds:
- Salaried applicants with income < ₹50,000/month: Max **50%**.
- Salaried applicants with income ₹50,000 – ₹1,50,000/month: Max **60%**.
- HNI Salaried applicants with income > ₹1,50,000/month: Max **65% to 70%**.
- Self-employed applicants: Max **60% to 65%** based on cash accruals.

## Loan to Value (LTV) Limits (RBI Mandated for Housing Loans)

| Loan Ticket Size | Maximum Permissible LTV | Risk Weight Assigned |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Up to ₹30 Lakh** | **90%** | 35% |
| **₹30 Lakh to ₹75 Lakh** | **80%** | 35% |
| **Above ₹75 Lakh** | **75%** | 50% |

*Note: In non-housing collateral (e.g. Loan Against Property), standard bank policy restricts LTV to 50% - 65% of certified market value.*
