# Relationship Intelligence & Contextual Customer Notes

## Unstructured Notes to Structured Intelligence
Core core banking systems (Finacle/Core Banking) store transactional numbers (balances, EMIs, account types), but miss qualitative relationship intelligence:
- Client personality type (analytical, time-constrained, conservative vs aggressive investor).
- Non-financial milestones (children's graduation years, upcoming family real estate inheritances, planned relocations).
- Service friction points (past issues with debit card delivery, specific branch branch manager sensitivities).

## Logging Context in Saathi
RMs should regularly log post-meeting summaries into Saathi:
- **Tone & Sentiment**: Was the client satisfied with quarterly portfolio returns?
- **Actionable Next Steps**: Commitments made during informal chats.
- **Family Ecosystem**: Identifying cross-sell opportunities within extended family accounts.
This ensures that the bank owns the relationship capital rather than individual employee roasters.
