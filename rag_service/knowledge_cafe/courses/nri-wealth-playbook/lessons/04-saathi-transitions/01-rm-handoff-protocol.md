# RM Handoff Protocol & Relationship Continuity

## The Relationship Continuity Dilemma
Banking relationships are fundamentally personal. In wealth management, when a Relationship Manager departs or changes territory:
- Up to **35% of Total Relationship Value (TRV)** is at risk of leaking within 6 months.
- The new RM often starts from zero, asking clients repetitive questions about basic preferences, leading to client fatigue and frustration.

## The Standard Saathi Transition Protocol
1. **Context Ingestion**: Before contacting the client, the successor RM reviews the customer's Saathi Relationship Profile.
2. **Warm Handshake Call**: When possible, a joint introductory call or video session between the outgoing RM and incoming RM is scheduled.
3. **Implicit Memory Activation**: Acknowledging specific client nuances on day one (e.g. "I understand you prefer communicating over WhatsApp during London evening hours" or "I see we are preparing for your daughter's tuition remittance in September") instantly builds trust and proves institutional memory.
