# Mortgage Floating Rate Resets & Portfolio Dynamics

## Understanding External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR)
Since October 2019, all floating-rate retail personal and home loans extended by commercial banks are mandated by RBI to be linked to an External Benchmark (predominantly RBI Policy Repo Rate).

$$\text{Effective Home Loan Rate} = \text{Repo Rate} + \text{Operating Spread} + \text{Credit Risk Premium}$$

When the Reserve Bank adjusts the benchmark repo rate, the change automatically transmits to the borrower's loan account at the next quarterly or reset cycle date.

## The Retention Vulnerability Window
1. When repo rate hikes accumulate, borrower EMI or tenure stretches significantly.
2. Competitor financial institutions aggressively target HNI accounts with balance transfer (BT) offers advertising teaser spreads that are 25 to 50 bps lower.
3. If an RM only notices when a foreclosure statement is requested, the customer is already 80% through the competitor's onboarding pipeline.
