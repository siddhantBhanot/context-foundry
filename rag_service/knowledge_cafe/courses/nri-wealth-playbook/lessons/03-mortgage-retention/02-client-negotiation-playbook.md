# Mortgage Retention & Counter-Offer Negotiation Playbook

## The Switching Cost Breakeven Framework
When an HNI borrower threatens a balance transfer to save 30-40 bps, RMs should walk the client through the actual friction costs:
1. **MODT Stamp Duty**: Memorandum of Deposit of Title Deeds stamp duty ranges from 0.2% to 0.5% of total loan value in states like Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Delhi. On a ₹3 Crore loan, this alone equals ₹60,000 to ₹1,50,000.
2. **Processing & Valuation Fees**: New lender upfront processing fee (₹15,000 to ₹50,000) plus legal title search and technical valuation fees.
3. **Turnaround & Document Retrieval**: 15 to 30 days of coordinating original property paper handovers from central repository vaults.

## The Internal Bank Spread Reset Solution
- **Action**: Offer an immediate internal spread modification.
- **Mechanism**: The customer pays a nominal administrative conversion fee (typically ₹2,500 to ₹5,000, which can be waived under Branch Head discretion for Burgundy / Burgundy Private clients).
- **Execution**: Completed completely paperless within 2 hours without moving title documents or re-stamping mortgages.
- **Result**: The borrower locks in competitive benchmark parity immediately without any out-of-pocket transfer loss.
