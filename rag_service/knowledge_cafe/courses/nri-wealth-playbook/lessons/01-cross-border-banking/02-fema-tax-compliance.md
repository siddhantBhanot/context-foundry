# FEMA & Tax Compliance in Cross-Border Wealth Advisory

## Determination of Residential Status
Under Section 6 of the Income Tax Act, 1961:
- An individual is a **Resident in India** if they are in India for 182 days or more during the financial year, or 60 days in that year and 365 days in the preceding 4 years.
- For Indian citizens leaving India for employment abroad, the 60-day rule is extended to 182 days.

## Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement (DTAA)
- Relationship managers must ensure clients submit:
  1. **Tax Residency Certificate (TRC)** issued by the government of their country of tax residence.
  2. **Form 10F** electronically filed on the Indian Income Tax e-filing portal.
  3. **No-PE Declaration** certifying no permanent establishment in India.
- Submitting valid DTAA documentation enables reduced withholding tax on NRO interest (typically 10% to 15% under treaties with USA, UK, Singapore, UAE, rather than the statutory 31.2% rate).
