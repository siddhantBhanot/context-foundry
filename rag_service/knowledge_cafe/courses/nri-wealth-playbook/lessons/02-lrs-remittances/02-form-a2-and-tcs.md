# Form A2 and Tax Collected at Source (TCS) Rules

## Form A2 Documentation & Processing
Every outbound remittance under LRS requires:
1. **Form A2**: Mandatory declaration signed by the remitter specifying the exact purpose code (e.g., S0305 for education tuition fees).
2. **PAN Card Verification**: Real-time PAN validation to check cumulative LRS utilization across all Authorised Dealer (AD) banks via RBI's LRS reporting portal.
3. **Student / University Documentation**: For education remittances, offer letter/I-20/CAS document and fee invoice from the foreign university.

## TCS Rate Structure (Section 206C(1G))

| Remittance Category | Threshold Limit | TCS Rate (Up to Threshold) | TCS Rate (Exceeding Threshold) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Education funded by 80E Bank Loan** | INR 7 Lakh | NIL | **0.5%** |
| **Education self-funded / Medical treatment** | INR 7 Lakh | NIL | **5.0%** |
| **Overseas Tour Program Packages** | No threshold | 5.0% (up to INR 7 Lakh) | **20.0%** |
| **Any Other Remittances (Investments, Gifts, Maintenance)** | INR 7 Lakh | NIL | **20.0%** |

### Relationship Manager Tip:
Remind clients that TCS is not an additional tax burden; it reflects in Form 26AS/AIS as advance tax credit and can be offset against annual domestic income tax liability or claimed as an income tax refund upon filing.
