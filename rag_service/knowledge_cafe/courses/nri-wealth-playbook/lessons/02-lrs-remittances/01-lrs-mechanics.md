# Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) Operational Mechanics

## Overview of LRS
Under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS), resident individuals (including minors) are permitted to freely remit up to **USD 250,000** per financial year (April – March) for any permissible current or capital account transactions, or a combination of both.

## Permissible Current Account Purposes
- Private visits abroad to any country (except Nepal and Bhutan).
- Gifts or donations to non-residents.
- Going abroad for employment or business travel.
- Maintenance of close relatives residing abroad.
- Medical treatment expenses abroad.
- Studies abroad (tuition fees, living expenses, university deposits).

## Capital Account Permissions
- Opening and maintaining foreign currency bank accounts overseas.
- Purchasing property abroad.
- Making investments in overseas equities, mutual funds, venture funds, or unlisted shares.
- Setting up wholly owned subsidiaries or joint ventures (ODI framework).
