# Regulatory Reporting: CTR, STR & FIU-IND Workflows

## Regulatory Reporting Schedule

| Report | Threshold / Trigger | Filing Deadline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cash Transaction Report (CTR)** | All cash transactions where value exceeds **INR 10 Lakhs** (or equivalent foreign currency), including integrally connected individual cash transactions totaling > ₹10 Lakh in a month | By **15th day of succeeding month** |
| **Suspicious Transaction Report (STR)** | Any transaction giving rise to reasonable ground of suspicion, irrespective of amount | Within **7 working days** of Principal Officer reaching satisfaction |
| **Cross-Border Wire Transfer (CWTR)** | All cross-border wire transfers exceeding **INR 5 Lakhs** | By **15th day of succeeding month** |
| **Counterfeit Currency Report (CCR)** | All cash deposits involving forged/counterfeit banknotes | By **15th day of succeeding month** |

## Tipping-Off Prohibition
Section 54 of PMLA strictly prohibits **tipping off**: under no circumstances may a bank employee inform or alert a customer that their account is under AML scrutiny or that an STR has been furnished to FIU-IND.
