# Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Typologies

## Stages of Money Laundering
1. **Placement**: Introducing illicit proceeds into the legitimate financial system (e.g. cash deposits).
2. **Layering**: Concealing the source through complex layers of financial transactions, wire transfers, and shell entities.
3. **Integration**: Returning laundered funds to the economy as apparently legitimate wealth or investments.

## Common Typologies & Red Flags
- **Structuring / Smurfing**: Breaking down large cash transactions below statutory reporting limits (e.g. multiple deposits of ₹49,000 to avoid mandatory PAN quote at ₹50,000, or just under ₹10 Lakh to evade CTR).
- **Mule Accounts**: Accounts operated by third parties (often students or economically vulnerable individuals) receiving sudden high-value RTGS/IMPS funds immediately withdrawn in cash or transferred to crypto platforms.
- **Dormant Account Reactivation**: Accounts dormant for years suddenly receiving high-velocity credits.
