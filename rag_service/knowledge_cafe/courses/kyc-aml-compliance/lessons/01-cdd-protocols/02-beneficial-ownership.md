# Beneficial Ownership Identification Rules

## Definition of Beneficial Owner (BO)
The Beneficial Owner is the natural person who ultimately owns or controls a client or the person on whose behalf a transaction is being conducted.

## Threshold Criteria (Rule 9 of PMLA Rules)
1. **Companies**: Any natural person who, whether acting alone or together through one or more juridical persons, has a controlling ownership interest of more than **10%** of shares or capital or profits of the company, or exercises control through other means.
2. **Partnership Firms**: Any natural person having ownership of / entitlement to more than **10%** of capital or profits of the partnership.
3. **Unincorporated Associations / Trusts**: Any natural person holding more than **15%** of property or capital or profits, or the author/trustee/beneficiary with 10% or more interest in the trust.

When no natural person meets the threshold, the natural person holding the position of senior managing official is identified as the Beneficial Owner.
