# Customer Due Diligence (CDD) & Video KYC Protocols

## RBI Customer Identification Standards
Customer Due Diligence (CDD) involves obtaining and verifying customer identity through reliable, independent sources before establishing a banking relationship.

## Key Identification Documents (OVDs)
Under RBI Master Direction - Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016:
- **Officially Valid Documents (OVD)**:
  1. Passport
  2. Driving Licence
  3. Proof of possession of Aadhaar number (with redaction of initial 8 digits if physical copy is received)
  4. Voter's Identity Card issued by the Election Commission of India
  5. Job card issued by NREGA duly signed by an officer of the State Government
  6. Letter issued by the National Population Register containing name and address
- **Permanent Account Number (PAN)**: Mandatory for all accounts; Form 60 must be obtained if PAN is not allotted.

## Video-based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP)
- Live audio-visual interaction conducted by an authorized bank officer.
- Real-time geo-tagging ensuring customer is physically located within the territorial borders of India.
- Liveness check, facial match against Aadhaar / PAN database using facial recognition algorithms.
- Validation of OTP transmitted during digital session.
