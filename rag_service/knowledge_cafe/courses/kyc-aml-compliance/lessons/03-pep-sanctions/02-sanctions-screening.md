# Sanctions Screening & Negative List Matching

## Regulatory Watchlists
Banks in India are mandated to screen all customers, beneficial owners, and transaction counterparties against:
1. **United Nations Security Council (UNSC) Sanctions Lists**: Specifically Resolutions 1267/1989/2253 (ISIL/Al-Qaida) and 1988 (Taliban).
2. **Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) Unlawful Activities Prevention Act (UAPA)** notified terrorist lists.
3. **Global Sanctions Lists**: OFAC (US Treasury), EU Sanctions, and UK HMT for all cross-border SWIFT wire remittances.

## Positive Match Escalation
If a screening hit occurs:
- Immediate freeze on all debit transactions.
- Zero tipping off to the customer.
- Urgent notification to the Principal Officer and FIU-IND within 24 hours.
