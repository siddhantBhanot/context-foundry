# Politically Exposed Persons (PEP) Governance

## Definition of PEP
Politically Exposed Persons are individuals who are or have been entrusted with prominent public functions in a foreign country or domestically, including:
- Heads of State or Governments.
- Senior politicians and political party leaders.
- Senior government, judicial or military officers.
- Senior executives of state-owned corporations and central banks.
- Close family members (spouse, children, parents, siblings) and known close associates of such persons.

## Onboarding Protocols for PEPs
1. **Senior Management Approval**: No PEP account may be opened without prior written approval from an official of the rank of General Manager or higher.
2. **Source of Wealth & Source of Funds**: Detailed verification of how the client acquired total net worth, backed by tax filings, audited financial statements, and business documentation.
3. **Continuous Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)**: Accounts flagged as PEP remain on an automated watchlist subjected to quarterly transaction reviews by Compliance.
